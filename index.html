3DSpotify
Install Guide

Four steps to get Spotify streaming on your 3DS — homebrew required.

1
Download & Install 3DSpotify
Prerequisite: Your 3DS must have custom firmware (CFW) installed — e.g. Luma3DS — and the Homebrew Launcher accessible. If you haven't done that yet, visit 3ds.hacks.guide first.
Latest Releases on GitHub
Open FBI → Remote Install → Scan QR Code
2
Create a Spotify Developer App
  • Go to developer.spotify.com/dashboard and log in with your Spotify account (free or premium both work).
  • Click Create App. Give it any name and description (e.g. "3DSpotify").
  • In the Redirect URIs field, enter exactly:
    http://127.0.0.1:5501/index.html
    then click Add and save.
  • Open your new app's settings. Copy the Client ID and Client Secret — you'll paste them below.
Open Spotify Dashboard →
3
Generate Your Auth QR Code

Paste your credentials from the Spotify dashboard, then click Authorize. You'll be redirected to Spotify to approve access — after that, your combined QR code will appear below.

Your QR Code is Ready

This single code contains your Client ID, Secret, and Refresh Token.

4
Scan the QR Code in 3DSpotify
Open 3DSpotify on your 3DS. Navigate to the Settings tab, then choose Scan QR Code. Point the camera at the QR code generated in Step 3. Your credentials will load automatically and the app will connect to Spotify.
